# 📊 TL Workhub Project (RealPage)

**Role:** Operations Manager / Workflow Designer  
**Tech Stack:** Salesforce APIs, Web Dashboard, GitHub  

---

## 📖 Overview
TL Workhub is an **operational command center for Customer Support Team Leaders**. It consolidates Salesforce case data into a leadership dashboard for backlog management, risk governance, workload monitoring, coaching, and daily decision‑making.

---

## 🎯 Goals
- Provide Team Leads with one shared view of active workload.  
- Reduce reliance on scattered reports and spreadsheets.  
- Surface P1/P2 cases and risks requiring leadership intervention.  
- Improve backlog visibility, ownership, and accountability.  
- Support coaching and governance workflows in one workspace.  

---

## 🔑 Key Capabilities
- **Consolidated backlog view** across teams, managers, agents, priorities, and products.  
- **P1/P2 governance** with dedicated risk visibility.  
- **Unassigned‑case monitoring** to close ownership gaps.  
- **Historical backlog trends** for workload analysis.  
- **Case drill‑downs** from metrics to individual Salesforce records.  
- **Filters, search, export** for focused reviews and reporting.  
- **Coaching metrics** to identify workload imbalances and enablement needs.  

---

## 🛠️ Contributions
- Designed and deployed the leadership dashboard in production.  
- Implemented multi‑dimensional filtering, backlog visualizations, and drill‑downs.  
- Coordinated rollout with Team Leads and refined workflows based on feedback.  
- Established governance metrics for risk and escalation management.  

---

## 📈 Outcomes
- Faster identification of critical cases and risks.  
- Reduced time spent combining reports and spreadsheets.  
- Improved backlog ownership and accountability.  
- More consistent leadership decisions and coaching interventions.  
- Enhanced Team Lead productivity and customer outcomes.  

---

## 🚀 Status
TL Workhub is **live in production** and actively used by Team Leaders. Current focus is on enhancements to reporting, governance workflows, and leadership metrics.

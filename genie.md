# 🧩 Genie Project (RealPage)

**Role:** Operations Manager / AI Workflow Designer  
**Tech Stack:** Python, Salesforce REST API, SOQL, JavaScript, OpenAI APIs, GitHub  

---

## 📖 Overview
Genie is an **AI-powered CRM and intelligent operations workspace** designed for RealPage Customer Support agents. It integrates with Salesforce to provide case prioritization, account health insights, and AI-assisted resolution guidance — helping agents spend less time searching for information and more time resolving customer issues.

---

## 🎯 Goals
- Replace flat case lists with a prioritized daily workspace.  
- Surface high-risk cases first.  
- Reduce manual searching across Salesforce and knowledge sources.  
- Improve troubleshooting consistency and first-contact resolution.  
- Provide AI-assisted workflows while keeping agents in control.  

---

## 🔑 Key AI Components
- **PrioritySense AI** → Automated case prioritization with confidence scoring and agent feedback.  
- **Operational Insight AI** → Account health lens showing risk signals, sentiment, and renewal status.  
- **Case Summary AI** → Synthesized complex case data into agent-ready summaries.  
- **Smart Action AI** → Generated recommended resolution steps with validation and feedback capture.  

---

## 🛠️ Contributions
- Designed and tested AI components for prioritization, context enrichment, and resolution guidance.  
- Implemented **data normalization and enrichment** across Salesforce records.  
- Established governance and feedback loops to continuously improve AI recommendations.  
- Coordinated prototype rollout and validation with support agents.  

---

## 📈 Outcomes
- Faster case understanding and prioritization.  
- Reduced duplicate investigations and manual searching.  
- Improved reuse of known workarounds.
